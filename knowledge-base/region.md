# Meridia Region

Inspired, but only lightly, by Chile and its diversity, as well as focus on tradition, but also some of its weaknesses, like the importance of status. The region had a conflict in the past that was resolved several years ago by the current Champion.
Aside from that, it's mainly meant to be a fictional place full of wonder and adventure, not a critique of real world politics.
The cities names are themed after geographical features. The city has central big cities, but also small and somewhat forgotten smaller locations.

## Organizations:

### The Wardens
The armed branch of the Pokemon League, and Champion Elias personal guard. They act as an heroic group, saving the region from threats, specially from Team Ashen.

### Team Ashen
A mob-like evil organization that usually steals and causes chaos.

## Notable Places
- Haven Town: The town where the main trio grew up. It's a quiet, traditional town, but it has a few secrets. The most important family is Cedric's, a family of elite trainers.
