# Gyms

## Gyms order

1. Flying: In a city by the sea coast, with strong winds and fishermen.
2. Grass: In a city in the middle of a forest area. This happens after the mc has been to the capitol city by train, so it's a comeback to nature and tradition.
3. Ground: In a mining area by the far north. The place is a bit isolated, hard working people.
4. Electric: Another coastal city, but this one is more touristic, modern.
5. Ghost: In an island full of myths and traditions. Perhaps a bit "Caleuche" influenced.
6. Ice: In the far south, a place with scientists doing research.
7. Steel: In the capitol city. It's supposed to be a challenge and set the idea of challenging the elite 4.
8. Fairy: In an isolated natural zone in the south, a bit touristic, the nature/adventure kind, but the gym itself should be more about tradition and mystery.

## Gym Leaders:
Flying - Captain Ivo: A former rescue pilot turned harbor master. He’s broad-shouldered, weathered, practical, and deeply respected by the fishing community. He treats Flying-types not as symbols of freedom, but as tools of vigilance: scouting storms, carrying lines, locating missing boats.
Role: First challenge, a glimpse into the idea that true heroism isn't about being in the spotlight.

Grass - Lina: A young ethnobotanist and activist who returned from the capital after becoming disillusioned with League-approved development. She protects local forests from overuse and is suspicious of institutions. Some locals admire her, others see her as disruptive.
Role: Presents the idea of rediscovering traditions.

Ground - Solea: A former geologist for the League who left after a mining collapse was quietly blamed on workers instead of negligent oversight. She now runs the gym while also documenting seismic activity and old underground ruins.
Role: Hints at the legendary.

Electric - Marin: A civic engineer and public figure who helped turn her coastal city into a modern tourist hub powered by new infrastructure and dazzling spectacle. She is stylish, articulate, and always seems in control, treating Electric-types as symbols of innovation, efficiency, and momentum. Beneath her warmth is a very sharp instinct for status, presentation, and control.
Role: Presents the idea of modernity as something glamorous, useful, and subtly superficial.

Ghost - Nico: A young islander who inherited the gym but feels trapped by expectations to preserve its customs. He loves the island, but resents being turned into part of its folklore. He’s skeptical, defensive, and a little bitter.
Role: Bonds with Oscar and deepens on the idea of belonging and purpose.

Ice - Serac: A former Warden, he disobeyed Elias and was sent here as punishment, but he's found purpose instead. He is stern, disciplined, and difficult to read, but not unkind.
Role: Thickens the plot around the Wardens. He introduces the idea that some truths are frozen beneath official narratives.

Steel - Isolde: A refined industrial magnate and patron of civic projects in the capital. Publicly philanthropic, elegant, and deeply connected to elite circles. She represents status, influence, and the polished metal face of the city.
Role: Mostly to set the tone. Oscar is underestimated and discouraged, but triumphs.

Fairy - Ysela: A guardian of an isolated southern sanctuary where old rituals, oral traditions, and natural paths are still preserved. She welcomes visitors, but only those willing to approach with patience and humility. She uses Fairy-types not as symbols of cuteness, but of mystery, memory, and the unseen ties between people and place.
Role: Brings the themes of tradition, belonging, and the region's forgotten spiritual roots into focus. She helps prepare Oscar for the legendary conflict, not by giving answers directly, but by teaching him how to see what others ignore.
