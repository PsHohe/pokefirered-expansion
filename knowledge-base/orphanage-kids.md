# Orphanage Kids

These are the NPCs that live in the orphanage. They may be quest givers at some points of the story.

## Teo (7yo)
Teo is a restless, playful boy with scraped knees, messy hair, and endless confidence. He treats the orphanage backyard like a wilderness to conquer, turning trees, broken fences, and old play structures into “forts” and “battlefields.” He has a habit of exaggerating everything, especially his own bravery, but his excitement is sincere and infectious. Beneath all that energy, he’s the kind of kid who gets attached quickly and wants badly to prove he can keep up with the older children.

## Iris (8yo)
Iris is gentle, cheerful, and endlessly charmed by anything she finds lovely, whether it’s flowers, bugs with pretty wings, or stories about elegant Pokémon. She often decorates her little corner outside with petals, stones, and handmade trinkets, treating it like a tiny secret garden. While she can seem dreamy or childish at first, she actually has a very good memory and a strong sense of attachment to people and places. She dislikes conflict, but when upset, she becomes quietly stubborn and refuses to let things go.

## Thomas (10yo)
Thomas is a friendly, talkative boy who is endlessly curious about Pokémon, especially their habits, diets, and odd little behaviors that most trainers ignore. His side of the dormitory is usually cluttered with handwritten notes, sketches, and improvised “research,” and he gets excited whenever someone returns from outside with a strange feather, footprint story, or rumor about a rare species. He dreams of becoming a Pokémon Professor someday, not because he wants fame, but because he genuinely believes understanding Pokémon is more important than simply battling them. He can become so absorbed in his questions that he forgets when other people are not keeping up.

## Vera (11yo)
Vera is one of the oldest children in the orphanage and carries herself with a level-headed, almost protective maturity. She often sits by the TV less because she cares about the programs and more because she likes staying informed and keeping an eye on what’s happening around her. She is calm, perceptive, and a little dry in her humor, with a tendency to speak plainly rather than sweetly. Though she can come across as distant, she has a strong sense of responsibility toward the younger kids and notices more than she lets on.
