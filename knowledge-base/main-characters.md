# Characters:

## Oscar (Main character): 

Age: 10-11
Personality: The main character, 10-11yo. Has lived in an orphanage in Haven Town his entire life. As a silent hero, not a lot of his personality will show, but some core points is that he's independent, quiet, morally stubborn.
Team: Open to the player.

## Adrian:

Age: 12-13
Backstory: Used to live in the orphanage with Oscar. He became a cadet in The Wardens through hard work. He's now a promising cadet, rapidly earning the attention of Champion Elias.
Personality: Disciplined and hardworking, very protective of others, but can be a little condescending, but not intentionally.
Role: Rival, but friendly. Oscar's best friend, he wants Oscar to follow him in joining The Wardens. While he doesn't see him as often now, he still has a subtle savior complex for him and wants what's best for him.
Team: Prefers common, but strong pokemon, his team has synergy, a bit defensive.
Ace: Corviknight + starter with advantage over the player

## Cedric

Age: 10-11
Backstory: Lives in Haven Town, the son of a family of strong trainers, somewhat rich. His family is very competitive, and expects him to excel in everything, like his siblings before him.
Personality: Arrogant, theatrical, witty, sharp-tongued, competitive, but secretly insecure.
Role: Rival, in a traditional way. He will challenge Oscar often and intends to prove his superiority.
Team: Prefers rare pokemon, his team is traditionally competitive.
Ace: Dragapult + starter with weakness against the player's


## Champion Elias

Age: Mid 30s
Backstory: Became the Champion as a teenager and saved the region from an evil organization. Since then, he's been undefeated and the face of hope.
Personality: Charismatic, magnetic, gracious, calm. He always remains positive. Secretly, he's narcissistic, but he doesn't show it easily.
Role: Champion. He has allowed Team Ashen to prosper as a way to .
Team: Strong, balanced team, the ultimate challenge in this game.
Ace: Volcarona


## Professor Elena Myrtle

Age: Late 20s to late 30s
Backstory: The region’s leading Pokémon researcher. She specializes in how Pokémon adapt to different climates and terrains across the region. She is well-respected by the League, though not deeply involved in its political side. She has known Elias for years and publicly supports the League, but privately has grown uneasy with how much influence The Wardens have accumulated.
Personality: Intelligent, observant, patient, grounded. She is warm with children and young trainers, but not overly sentimental. She values honesty, curiosity, and restraint. She is difficult to fool because she notices patterns quickly.
Role: Regional professor and one of the first adults to take Oscar seriously on his own merits. She acts as a quiet moral counterweight to Elias. As the story progresses, she begins to suspect that Team Ashen’s repeated returns make no sense unless someone powerful benefits from them.
Team: Not focused on formal battling, but she is clearly competent. If she ever fights, her team should reflect adaptability, unusual habitats, and fieldwork rather than raw strength.
Ace: Torterra


## Boss Vera

Age: Late 20s to mid 30s
Backstory: The leader of Team Ashen. Unlike the grunts beneath her, Vera is not driven by simple greed or chaos. She built Team Ashen into a feared criminal force through discipline, strategy, and a willingness to use fear as a weapon. Though Team Ashen is publicly seen as the eternal enemy of Elias and The Wardens, Vera’s relationship with Elias is far more complicated. The two maintain a secret pact, each using the other to preserve their own power. Vera knows Elias is exploiting the conflict to strengthen his image, but she accepts it because it gives her freedom to operate and pursue her own ambitions.
Personality: Cold, composed, intelligent, and intimidating. Vera rarely raises her voice, which makes her even more unsettling. She is sharp-eyed and difficult to deceive, with little patience for weakness or excuses. She believes fear reveals people’s true nature, and has no respect for those who rely on empty ideals. Unlike Elias, who hides his control behind grace and heroism, Vera is far more direct about power and cruelty.
Role: Boss of Team Ashen, and the apparent main villain for much of the story. She represents a more openly brutal form of power than Elias, making her the face of organized evil in the early and middle game. As the story progresses, it becomes clear that she is not merely serving Elias, but pursuing her own goals through their unstable alliance.
Team: Focused on pressure, disruption, and intimidation. Her Pokémon should feel oppressive and commanding, forcing the player onto the back foot. Her battles are meant to feel harsh and controlled, as though she is testing whether her opponent will break under pressure.
Ace: Honchkrow
